# Playtest 2026-10-27, agent1 (sandbox, 5 game years)

Save: `saves/agent1.pkl`. About 75 CLI calls. I played only through the CLI and `docs/play.md`.

## 1. What I built and how it went

Layout, all reached from the entry at (166,169):
- **Core block** (x140-166, y150-160): a grid of 2x2 houses between roads y=150/155/160. A flower-garden row at y=148 and garden columns inside the block. Services along y=161: fountain, maintenance, podium, food vendor.
- **Farm spine** on the meadow along road x=150 (y=108-149): wheat/carrot/onion farms, goat farms, hunting lodges, sheep farms, growers' lodges, 4 oil presses, 3 granaries, 3 storehouses. Side roads at y=111/115/123/127.
- **NE block** (x155-180, y135-147), the **south block** (x133-165, y171-177), and in year 4 a **north district** (x170-200, y98-112) with services built into the house rows.
- **Culture**: 4 theatres, 4 stadiums, 6 gymnasiums, about 10 podiums, 3 infirmaries.

At the end there were 183 houses and income of about +340/month (taxes 691, wages 356 at 11% tax). Food was in surplus; granaries were full from year 2. Unemployment was about 36% (722 of 2019). Many houses stayed at Hovel because of fleece and culture coverage gaps and desirability.

Phases:
- **Year 1**: worker shortage, then food ran out before the first July harvest. Sentiment fell from 63 to 43. Lowering tax and adding food vendors brought it back.
- **Year 2**: fast evolution to Tenement/Apartment, but tax income was tiny until I raised tax from 9% to 12-15%.
- **Years 3-5**: unemployment stayed high and fires kept destroying houses in blocks that maintenance walkers did not reach.

## 2. Bugs (with repro)

1. **`clear` over a tree crashes, and the whole `do` batch is lost** (nothing is saved):
   ```
   python3 -m sim new /tmp/r.pkl
   python3 -m sim do /tmp/r.pkl "clear 125 150 126 150"
   -> OverflowError: Python integer -2 out of bounds for uint32  (game.py clear(), flags &= ~0x1)
   ```
   This means trees cannot be cleared at all, so tree-covered land can never be used.
2. **Wheat farm builds on non-meadow land**, while a carrot farm next to it is correctly refused:
   ```
   python3 -m sim do /tmp/r.pkl "build wheat_farm 151 108" "build carrot_farm 147 108"
   python3 -m sim info /tmp/r.pkl 151 109   # tile (151,109): land
   ```
   (`goat_farm 136 125` also looked like it was on partly non-meadow land.)
3. **Buildings with no road access can be placed with no warning**: `build hunting_lodge 147 141` next to road x=150 with a gap column, and `build gymnasium 169 163`. `info` shows `access None`. A gymnasium placed this way built up 80 damage in a month while useless.
4. **Map digit is off by one from `info` level**: `look` shows `3` for a Hovel, which `info` reports as "level 2", and `7` for a Townhouse (level 6). The legend says "digit = level". Compare `look saves/agent1.pkl 138 148 32 16` with `info saves/agent1.pkl 141 151`.
5. **Stale or wrong names in `info`**:
   - Every house header reads `House1 Hut #N` whatever its level, e.g. "House1 Hut #61 … Townhouse (level 6)".
   - Maintenance shows as `Super Office #275` (`info saves/agent1.pkl 136 179`).
6. **"no storage with food/fleece/olive_oil" is reported for vendors that hold hundreds of units**: `info 167 156` shows `goods {'fleece': 453}` with status "no storage with fleece". The top-level `storage:` line read `-` for the whole first year although food was flowing.
7. **`do` exits with code 1 if any one command fails, but the other commands are still applied and saved.** This is inconsistent with bug 1, where a crash loses everything. Example: `do ... "road 160 178 163 178" ...`.
8. **`road` refuses the whole segment if one tile is off-map or a tree** (`road blocked at (163,178): off map`) instead of building up to the obstacle. This is minor, but it surprised me.
9. The legend repeats `3 house (digit = level, h = empty lot)  4 house (...)  5 house (...)` once per digit.

## 3. Confusing or missing information

- **The sentiment `parts` are per-month drift, not levels.** That is not explained anywhere. I only worked it out from history: the sum of parts is about the monthly change. A sentiment of 44 with parts summing to -1 looks like nonsense until you know this. Showing a target/equilibrium value would help.
- **`houses:` needs are not tied to locations**: "9 x needs desirability 30 (has 26)" does not say which houses. I had to `info` houses one by one. A `houses` command, or a `look` overlay for needs/desirability/coverage, would help a lot.
- **There is no way to see walker coverage**: which houses a fountain, tax collector or vendor actually reaches. Houses lost water or tax coverage when I added roads elsewhere, presumably because walker routes changed. `tax_days 0` on a block next to a staffed tax office was a mystery.
- **Maintenance does not appear in `coverage`**, and fire/damage risk is only visible per building. I lost a theatre, about 10 houses and nearly a stadium (damage 640) without seeing it coming. Burn and collapse events are only briefly in `recent`.
- **Desirability ranges are not explained**: `des=8/3` in the catalog is presumably value/range, but how it falls off with distance is not documented. In practice a garden 2 rows away gave 20 and 4 rows away gave 0.
- **No summary of jobs and production**: I wanted total jobs vs workers, production/consumption per good, and food months remaining.
- `storage:` sums all storage, but not per building or capacity, so "no place to deliver wheat" (granaries full) came as a surprise.
- `months N` prints only the new date. A short digest (events, money delta) would save a `status` call each time.

## 4. Balance problems

- **Fire and collapse are very fast and harsh.** A new theatre collapsed within about 3 months, and a stadium reached damage 640 within 2 months, when no maintenance walker passed by. Houses burned repeatedly in a block with 3 maintenance buildings nearby.
- **Tax income is tiny at the default 9%**: 35 dr/month for 444 people, which barely covers wages. Raising tax to 15% only cost -3/month sentiment and gave 5x the income. The default seems far too low, or the tax penalty too mild.
- **Wages are negligible** (about 0.27 dr per worker per month), so jobs cost almost nothing. Unemployment (-3/month) then becomes the main drag on sentiment, and the only remedy is to build buildings just to employ people. With no trade, extra producers just fill granaries.
- **Evolution is very fast**: Hut to Apartment in under a year. Devolution with evictions is equally fast whenever a walker misses a house for a month, so levels oscillate.
- **Food**: the first harvest takes a year, which is harsh; hunting lodges and goat farms carried me. From year 2, food was oversupplied, with all granaries full.
- **Goods hoarding**: each vendor holds 400-750 fleece or oil while houses nearby still "need fleece". Distribution depends on walker luck, not supply.
- **Pop growth stalls** at about 60/month with 1500 vacancies and sentiment 58 (migration +50%).

## 5. Missing features (vs. Zeus)

- No trade, imports or exports, so surplus goods and food have no use and there is no way to earn money other than taxes.
- No grapes or vineyard producer, although `winery` and `wine_vendor` exist (the winery needs grapes).
- No gods, sanctuaries, heroes, military or monsters (expected for a sandbox, but worth noting). Hazards are only fire, collapse and theft.
- No priority or worker allocation, no road blocks to steer walkers, no overlays (desirability, water, fire risk, crime).
- No undo or refund for `clear`: demolishing gives back nothing.
- No bridges or terrain clearing (trees cannot be cleared because of bug 1).

## 6. Top 5 recommended changes

1. **Fix the `clear` crash on trees** and make `do` atomic (all or nothing), or at least save what succeeded and report the failure.
2. **Add a coverage/needs view**: per-house needs with coordinates, and a `look` overlay for water, maintenance, food and goods coverage and desirability. Also explain the sentiment `parts` as monthly deltas.
3. **Reject or warn on buildings without road access** (and on farms off meadow, including wheat). Fix the level digit and the "House1 Hut" / "Super Office" naming.
4. **Tone down fire/collapse speed**, or put "at risk" buildings in `problems`, and make burn/collapse events stick in a log (a `log` or `events` command).
5. **Rebalance the economy**: a higher default tax or a smaller penalty, meaningful wages, a way to sell surplus (trade), and less vendor hoarding, so supply in storage actually reaches houses.
