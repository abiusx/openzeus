## (1) BUGS / inconsistencies
1. Food distribution: `status`/`report husbandry` show 3200 wheat in granary #39 (163,128) in July, but food vendor #11 (168,112) kept `goods {}` for months while houses went hungry. Repro: `info 168 112` vs `info 163 128` (after July harvest). Distance is 16 tiles.
2. `clear 163 158 166 161` on rubble: "cleared 0 buildings, 0 road tiles, 16 trees (cost 32)". Rubble counted as trees.
3. `build granary 163 158` on rubble: refused "(163,158) is rubble", yet docs say build over rubble. Only `clear` fixes it (see 2).
4. Olives: `info 165 118` growers_lodge goods {'olives': 64}; `info 165 132` oil_press "needs olives" for months. Olives do not reach the press.
5. Oil vendor: `problems: no storage with olive_oil within 40 tiles: oil_vendor#12` while storehouse #29 (168,138) held olive_oil 100 two months earlier.
6. Possibly skipped level: the Apr 999 BC snapshot shows Hovel 14, Tenement 3, Homestead 0 (not proven; a Homestead may have passed between snapshots). Check `history` at finer steps.
7. `build olive_tree` prints "WARNING: no road access" (trees do not need roads; misleading).
8. `build fruit_lodge`, `build bibliotheke`: refused as Poseidon buildings with Zeus only default. Fine per docs, but the fruit lodge is the only early food source besides farms, so it matters for balance.
9. Batch behaviour: a failing line in `do` prints earlier "built ..." lines but saves nothing. I did not notice until I checked with `info` (fountain, lodge, 3 olive trees lost). A status line or a "rolled back" note would help.
10. `undo` is one level only. Within one batch, two `undo`s reverted only the last build (expected), but the output says "4 built removed" for an agora with 3 stalls; fine, just confusing.
11. Tribute message: "Tribute paid by Elis ... unable to accept my tribute of 4 wine" with no wine handling set up (day 10). Odd message for an unhandled gift.
12. Athena event: "a sacred grove will grace your city" (olives) — no visible follow-up I could find; unclear.
13. `status` said "immigration limited by: none" and "migration +75%" with 48 vacancies and population flat for 2 months; the new houses were ~70 tiles from the entry, so the immigrants were walking. The status does not explain the walk delay.
14. Olympian Games message appeared with no visible effect (no athletes trained). Event text only.

## (2) BALANCE
1. Food is the bottleneck. Wheat is harvested once a year (July). With ~400 people the city eats ~100-150/month, so the city starves for ~4 months each year unless it stores a full year beforehand. Hungry houses hold their level only for a while; they devolve and "8 people evicted" follows. Recommend an early granary buffer or a faster second food.
2. Houses devolve fast. Hovel and Shack lost levels within 1-2 months when hungry or in fire-prone areas. Sentiment stayed near 72 while 18-21 houses were hungry, then dropped to 61 with unemployment; the sentiment effect of hunger looks small.
3. Fire and collapse: a granary built in April collapsed in 2 months with no maintenance. A house in the north caught fire in July before any maintenance was within reach (the only maintenance post, 165,140, was about 30 tiles from the north row; not measured). Maintenance coverage per walker felt too thin for a 228-tile map.
4. Labour: 42% of residents work, so every farm (10 staff), granary (18) and venue needs a lot of housing. Culture venues (gymnasium 7, podium 4) got no staff until culture priority 5; with 8 farms the city could not staff culture. Labour was the limiting factor for culture, which then blocked evolution.
5. Culture needs: houses required 15 then 35 culture (Hovel->Homestead, etc.) with only podium, gymnasium, college (Poseidon bibliotheke refused). This was achievable but tight.
6. Taxes: 9% gave only ~10-13 dr/month from 30-40 houses; wages ran ~50/month. Treasury is fine for now, but taxes look too low to pay for a city at 500+ people.
7. Desirability: most houses needed 10-20 desirability. Flower gardens (40 dr, +8) and sundials helped, but tax office, storehouse, granary and oil press pulled it down to 0 or below near them.
8. Early timing: the entry road was fine; the first 6 huts + farm + granary + food vendor needed a full agora (50 dr) and the vendor needs stalls. The agora rule (3x6 strip, one food vendor per agora) made the first food vendor cost 70 dr and a 40-tile reach limit, which surprised me.
9. Immigration: 96 vacancies filled at ~+50 people per month. Immigration was never the limit; food and housing devolution were.
