# Playtest report: sandbox, Normal, new game (city.pkl)

## Result
- Goal NOT reached (all common houses at Townhouse for 2 years).
- All 51 houses reached Townhouse for the first time Feb 988 BC (year 12); all were at Townhouse from Dec 987 to Sep 986 (about 10-12 months). Dec 986 they fell (41/51) after my storehouse clearing (see BUGS 7). Not held 24 months.
- Stopped at Jun 984 BC (game year ~16.5), about 515 commands, because the east side's oil and fleece supply is structural (see below), not a tuning problem I could fix in the remaining budget.

## Final numbers (Jun 984 BC)
- Population 2500 (about 2850 in 986); 51 houses: Townhouse 47, Hovel 4.
- Treasury 11.5k (tax 9%, taxes ~260/mo, wages ~205/mo, wage 30).
- Food stored 21,900 (35 months at 625/mo eaten): wheat 14.6k, carrots 3.2k, onions 3.6k. Food was never short after Apr 997.
- Workforce 1050, jobs 822, unemployed 228; sentiment 46 (migration 0 since Sep 990).
- Checkpoints (log.md): Jan 996 pop 1720; Jan 994 pop 2086 (14 Townhouse); Jan 992 pop 1966 (23); Jan 990 pop 2767 (45); Jan 988 pop 2687 (51).

## Hardest
1. Food in the first winter: pop 528 on 500 food/yr; houses devolved (14 of 41). Fixed by farms + a request; later food was never short.
2. Fleece and olive oil distribution to far houses. One peddler per agora and one vendor per good per agora; houses 25+ tiles from an agora starve of fleece/oil and devolve. This drove most of the churn (north row, west column, east column, spur).
3. Desirability and culture for Townhouse (see BALANCE). Solved with many decor pieces (interior, no road needed) and a stadium.
4. Unemployment 200-400 for years (jobs ~800 vs workforce 1100); sentiment stuck at 40-46.

## (1) BUGS / unexplained (exact commands, output)

Same request to ally Tiryns (`request Tiryns fleece`) did arrive as an offer (`6: Tiryns grants Fleece: 16 fleece`). Rival request silently lost?
2. Sheep farm (165,151), 8/8 staffed, 2-4 sheep placed Aug-Nov 999: fleece appeared once (storage fleece 100, Jul 998), then `report husbandry` lists no fleece for ~1 year. Later "no place to deliver fleece: sheep_farm#148" implies it does produce. Unexplained gap; check sheep ready/graze rules.
3. `report husbandry` says "food stored: 0 (0 months)" while `report distribution` says "agoras: 3 holding 2053 units" and granaries empty (Apr 998). The stock count ignores agora stock.
4. `build fleece_vendor 176 116` -> "this agora already has a fleece_vendor". Rule (one vendor per good per agora) is not in play.md. Also `clear` of a vendor leaves an empty stall with no warning (I did this twice by mistake).
5. `build storehouse 148 161` -> "built storehouse #220 ... WARNING: no road access". Accepted a building with no road; it can never work.
6. `storage 191 109 wine empty` -> "nowhere to empty wine to". The winery kept filling storehouses (wine 4000 across 3 storehouses) with no buyer, so bays never free up. Storage deadlock; the only exit was to demolish the storehouse (goods lost). Wine has no sink in the city unless a wine vendor/trade exists.
7. The Dec 986 cascade: `storage 191 109 wine empty`, `olives empty`, `olive_oil accept` (then `clear` + rebuild of 191,109) -> olive oil stopped reaching the east agoras; 25 Townhouse/Tenement devolved; pop 2780 -> 2260. 8. `build X ...` batches fail randomly: "(187,149) has a walker on it (immigrant)", "(196,128) has a walker on it (homeless)". The homeless walker stood on a cleared tile for ~2 months (8+ attempts). The batch is all-or-nothing, so one blocked tile drops the whole batch.
9. Catalog lists Poseidon-only buildings as normal: `bibliotheke`, `bird_bath`, `laboratory`. Build error says "is a Poseidon building", which is correct, but `catalog` should mark them.
10. Plague: 171 deaths around Jun 993 with health 85 (target 83). Primer says plague risk below 75. `report hygiene` said "plague chance: none" a few months before. Unexplained.
11. Collapses without a maintenance walker nearby (events show "X collapsed"): granary (172,114) and two farms (months 3-5); drama school + theatre on the north road (5 months); fountains (205,119), (196,138), (180,151); house (182,148). Maintenance (2 offices) was not enough outside the ring. Not a bug per se, but no early warning other than `risk`.
12. `build sheep ...` / `build goat ...` often fails: "sheep must be placed on free meadow" for tiles that look like meadow in `look`. Fine, but hard to find free tiles; `look` does not show animals.
13. I ran `do ... "wolves 0 0"` by mistake (test command) on my save at ~Jun 998. Output suppressed. The save may carry a wolf pack at (0,0); no effect seen.

## (2) BALANCE
- Townhouse gates (from `info`): Hovel needs desirability 10, Tenement 20, Apartment 30; Townhouse culture 45 (dropped to 30 on the spur -> devolved). Decor is the main lever; stone circles (24, 4x4) and flower gardens (8, 2x2) worked. The gates feel high for a 2-3 year city.
- Desirability is also hurt by agoras (-12), granaries (-12), storehouses (-2), farms (-3 to -5), guardposts (-2), tax office (-4). Keeping houses near an agora kills their desirability.
- Culture: athletics (gym) and philosophy (podium needs a college) reach ~90%; drama needs a theatre AND a drama school (theatre alone 5-14% reach); competition needs a stadium (only one per city; 45 workers, 400 dr, excellent). Laboratory is Poseidon.
- Unemployment: artisans_guild (25 jobs, 75 dr, no inputs) is the cheapest job sink; granary (18 jobs, 100 dr). Still needed ~400 jobs more than we built; sentiment ~40-46 without it.
- Food is far too easy after year 2: 14-16k wheat stored with 6 wheat farms; "no place to deliver wheat" every year. Farms could be fewer.
- Taxes: Townhouses pay ~5-7 dr/month each; income 250-350/mo with 50 houses. Tax office reach (one office) was enough.
- Immigration: 0% once sentiment < 50 (about year 10). Vacancies then stayed 200-400.

## (3) PRIMER (docs/play-strategy.md)
Helped:
- "Store before you need it" and "grow housing before industry" (the first winter was the lesson).
- "Maintenance early and per district" (granary/farm collapses in months 3-5 were exactly this).
- "Culture: podium needs a college; theatre needs a drama school" (both verified by `report culture`).
- "Agora per block with all goods" (correct, but the rule that goods are per vendor was missing).
Missing or wrong:
- One vendor per good per agora; one peddler per agora; the 40-tile storage radius for vendor buyers. Far houses starve without a second agora. This is the main thing that would have saved me time.
- Storage deadlock: goods with no sink (wine) fill storehouses and block the goods you need. Set `refuse` on storehouses for surplus goods early.
- Decor needs no road access (interior stone circles and gardens worked). Useful; not in the primer.
- Desirability and culture gates for Townhouse (numbers above), and that Poseidon-only pieces are rejected (bird bath, bibliotheke, laboratory).
- Homeless walkers block tiles; wait a month or use another tile.
- Tiryns (ally) gave fleece by request in one month; Aulis gave 8 olive oil; Sparta (rival) gave nothing. Trade by request is worth a line.
