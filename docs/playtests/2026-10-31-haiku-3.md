# Playtest report: player3 (sandbox, Normal, gods/events/heroes on, testcmds off)

## Goal
NOT reached. Never had all common houses at Townhouse at once with the economy stable. Best: 28 of 36 Townhouse (Feb 990 BC).
Stopped at Aug 988 BC (about 12 game years, ~400 CLI calls). Save: zeus-player3/game.pkl. Full log: zeus-player3/log.md.

## Final numbers (Aug 988 BC)
- Population 1700 (peak ~2040 in Jan 991 BC). Workforce 765, employed 684, unemployed 81 (11%).
- Houses 36: Townhouse 21, Apartment 4, Tenement 1, Homestead 9, Shack 1. 9 need culture 30/35.
- Treasury 21,340 dr. Taxes 488/month at 15 %. Wages 193/month. Sentiment 100.
- Food: stored, not short (wheat ~4,700, onions 1,000, carrots 700 in storage). Fleece 3,300 in storage (see bug 2). Olive oil 100.
- Culture: philosophy 72 %, athletics 97 %, competition 66 %, drama 0 % of houses reached.

## What was hardest
1. The first 18 months: houses went Hut->Shack->Hovel and evicted 8 people per step. Population fell 412 -> 256 while jobs were built ahead of food.
2. Jobs versus people: population doubled faster than jobs. Unemployment swung 0 % -> 33 % -> 0 % several times.
3. Culture: Townhouse needs 45. Drama stayed at 0 %, so the south houses stuck at 30.
4. Olive oil: Apartment and Townhouse need it. Oil press lacked olives for ~3 years. Import comes only from Aulis, and the quota caps it (see bug 6).
5. Collapses: granary, oil press, college, drama school, palace and maintenance all collapsed at least once. Taxes stopped for ~4 months when the palace fell.

## (1) BUGS (candidate; check the exact output)
1. Drama 0 %. `python3 -m sim report SAVE culture` shows `drama  1 Theatre, 0 Laboratory  0%` for years. Theatre (161,134) 18/18 staffed and drama school (161,140) 10/10 staffed (`info`). No shows, no reach. Blocks Townhouse (needs culture 45) in the south.
2. Sheep farm stock never leaves. `info 158 120`: `goods {'fleece': 500}` (and later more), while `info 170 131` (storehouse, accepts fleece, get order set) shows `goods {}`. `status`: `no storage with fleece within 40 tiles: fleece_vendor#...`. Fleece only reached the agora from imports.
3. Palace collapse is silent. Palace (168,150) became rubble; `status` gave no problem line. Taxes were 0 for 4 months (`last month: {'taxes': 0 ...}`). Maintenance (165,152) had also collapsed.
4. Oil shortage is not reported in `problems`. Houses showed `needs olive_oil` and devolved; `problems` listed nothing about oil until a vendor's stock was gone.
5. `request Elis food`, `request Tiryns food`, `request Aulis food` all answer `can't request food: not possible`, with no reason. Only `request Sparta wheat` worked, and it cost attitude 27 -> 7 (Furious) when refused.
6. Oil import stops at the partner quota; no message says so. `trade 200 102 olive_oil import 400` gave 0 delivered for 3 months; `trade` shows Aulis `olive_oil 4/12` (quota) and the post `goods {}`. The limit 400 is not reachable.
7. Batches fail on walkers and on overlapping footprints with no hint. `build house 178 140` -> `(178,140) has a walker on it (immigrant)`, and `clear` on a 4x4 granary's tile silently removed the granary (`cleared 1 buildings`). The batch then drops everything. Suggest a warning in `clear`.
8. Sheep farm placed 2x2 at (157,120) had `access None`. `info` said so but `build` did not refuse it. Easy trap.
9. Granary (172,140) and oil press (152,138) collapsed within ~3 months of being built, with maintenance 0 (the south had no maintenance until I added it).

## (2) BALANCE
- Early food: no crop is carted until harvest (onion/carrot Apr, wheat Jul). A winter-long gap starved houses. The primer says this, but a new player will still lose half the population.
- Eviction on devolve (8 people per house) is brutal: 412 -> 256 in one month.
- Jobs vs pop: a Hut-to-Townhouse city of ~1,700 needs ~700 workers. Artisans guild (25 jobs for 75 dr) was the cheapest job source; 5 guilds were built and then 3 removed because they starved the oil press.
- Taxes: 15 % gives ~500/mo at 1,700 people. 20 % gives a sentiment -5 per half-month. 15 % was stable.
- Desirability: granary -12 and storage nearby pulled southern houses to 2-10. Gardens (12 dr) and obelisks (18-24 dr) on free odd rows fixed most of it cheaply.
- Commemorative monument (cost 0, des 30, radius 6) was useful: built after the 2,000 milestone.

## (3) PRIMER
Helped:
- "Food is the first wall" and "one peddler per agora": the third agora (184,128) fixed houses that were fed nothing for 6 months.
- Agora reach 40 tiles and the granary `get` orders: south agora (183,148) only worked after a granary within 40 tiles.
- Unemployment rules: the sentiment numbers were right. Wage 34 helped.
- "Storehouse accept/refuse" and food caps (accept LIMIT) kept carrot farms from stalling.

Missing or wrong:
- The harvest timing: say the veg harvest arrives 1-2 months after April (carts), so houses are hungry till ~June. Add "never let the first winter start with 0 stock: the first granary fills at the July wheat".
- Trade: say that partners cap imports (quota per year, e.g. Aulis olive_oil 12/yr) so oil needs its own press and trees early. "trade import 400" is not a fix.
- Do not put a grower's lodge and oil press on a road with no maintenance; they collapse.
- Warn about footprints: 4x4 granary, 5x5 theatre, 3x3 schools; `clear` on one tile removes the whole building.
- Culture: say that Townhouse needs 45 culture and that drama and competition need venues in each district, not just one. The stadium is one per city and far from the south.
- Taxes: palace must be checked each year (risk); tax income stops silently when it collapses.
